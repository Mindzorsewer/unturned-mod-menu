## 🧩 About

**Unturned Mod** is a comprehensive mod menu for Unturned 3.x featuring item spawner, teleport, ESP, speed multiplier, vehicle tools, no recoil, infinite stamina, and server browser filters.

Based on popular mods like **RocketMod**, **OpenMod**, and **Bundles**.

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Core Mods
- Item Spawner — spawn any item by ID or name
- Teleport — move to waypoints, players, or coordinates
- ESP — see players, zombies, and loot through walls
- Speed Multiplier — adjust movement speed (0.1x to 5x)
- No Recoil — perfect accuracy while shooting

### 🚗 Vehicle & World
- Vehicle Spawner — spawn any vehicle by ID
- Vehicle Fly — free-flight for vehicles
- Infinite Fuel — never run out of fuel
- No Fall Damage — survive any drop

### 🏋️ Survival & Training
- Infinite Stamina — sprint without limits
- Instant Craft — craft anything without materials
- Unlock All Skills — max out every skill tree
